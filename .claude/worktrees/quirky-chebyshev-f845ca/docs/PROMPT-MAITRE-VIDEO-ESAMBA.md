Visual identity lock:
- dark background and high-end digital environments
- neon blue system glow for base interface ambiance
- green for validation and success states
- red for alerts and critical risk states
- soft bloom, controlled reflections, realistic glass and metallic UI surfaces
- readable typography in French, crisp and high contrast

Global direction:
- no people, no faces, no hands, no characters
- only interfaces, environments, devices, dashboards, screens, workflow overlays
- cinematic transitions, clean parallax, soft depth of field
- smooth easing for all UI animations, no jitter, no chaotic camera except incident urgency moment
- maintain visual continuity across all scenes (same design language, same product identity)

From urban operational chaos to full orchestration and trust in one connected fleet ecosystem over a single day.

## Contraintes négatives (anti-dérive)

Negative prompt:
- no humans, no face, no hand, no body, no character
- no cartoon, no anime, no comic style, no low-poly look
- no messy layout, no distorted UI, no illegible text
- no random logos, no brand drift, no inconsistent typography
- no excessive camera shake, no strobing, no aggressive flicker
- no over-saturated colors, no blown highlights, no crushed shadows
- no noisy artifacts, no compression glitches, no warped geometry
- no irrelevant objects, no futuristic weapons, no sci-fi characters

Stability rules:
- keep E-Samba visual language consistent across all scenes
- maintain dark UI baseline with blue system glow and semantic green/red states
- keep text in French and preserve semantic meaning scene to scene
- preserve interface proportions and iconography consistency
